How it works

  1. Free audit

    Send us your store's web address. Within 2 business days you get a short report showing which specs are missing on your equipment pages.

  2. Specs filled in

    Export your products from Shopify as a CSV file and send it over. In 5 business days we fill in specs, product category, FAQs and clearer titles, for your equipment pages or your whole catalog.

  3. Your review

    You get an import-ready CSV and a before-and-after report. Nothing changes in your store until you import it.

Where the data comes from

Every value comes from the manufacturer's spec sheet or from your own pages. When we can't confirm a spec, we leave it blank and flag it for you. We don't guess, because a wrong spec turns into a return.

Questions

What's in the free audit?

A short report on up to 10 of your equipment pages, showing which specs each page leaves out. It tells you what's missing; filling it in is the paid work.

Do you need access to my Shopify store?

No. A product CSV export is enough. If you'd rather not export, you can add us as a collaborator with access limited to products.

Will my product pages look different?

Your theme stays the same. The specs go into your product descriptions and product fields, in the same CSV format Shopify exports, so you see every change before you import it.

What if you can't find a spec?

We leave it blank and list it in the report, so you can fill it in yourself or ask the manufacturer.

Which stores do you work with?

Shopify stores in the United States with product pages in English.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe, or by PayPal invoice, in US dollars. You pay upfront, once you approve the proposal.
